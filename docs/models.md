## Specific checks requested

**Gemma 4 is Apache 2.0: confirmed.** The model card metadata says `license: apache-2.0`, and its licence link (`ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/gemma_4_license`) resolves to Google's "Apache License 2.0" page. This differs from Gemma 1–3, which used the Gemma Terms of Use.

**Gemma 4 sizes: E2B, E4B, 12B, 26B A4B, 31B.** The card says "available in five distinct sizes". 26B A4B is a Mixture-of-Experts model (25.2B total, 3.8B active). **There is no Gemma 4 27B.** ARCHITECTURE.md named "Gemma 4 27B" as a pool C candidate in two places; both now read "Gemma 4 31B (or 26B A4B)". The "Gemma 4 12B" translation upgrade in sections 2 and 3 exists as written.

**VoxCPM2 is 2B, Apache 2.0, and lists German and Arabic: confirmed.** The card says "2B parameters, 30 languages" and "Apache-2.0 license, free for commercial use". Its language list includes `de` and `ar`, and every other launch language. Safetensors metadata reports 2.29B parameters; at fp16 that is about 4.6 GB, in line with the "about 5 GB" in section 4.

## Contradictions and risks for the architect

1. **Gemma 4 27B does not exist** (factual error, corrected in ARCHITECTURE.md sections 3 and 7). Choosing between 31B dense and 26B A4B MoE for pool C is a design decision left open. Section 5's pool C throughput line still says "a 27B int4 model processes roughly 2,000 to 3,000 input tok/s". That is a planning figure, not a model name, so it was not edited. It should be restated once the pool C model is chosen.
2. **VoxCPM2 speed on an L4 is unproven and may limit pool A.** The card quotes a real-time factor (RTF) of about 0.30 on an RTX 4090, or about 0.13 with Nano-vLLM. It gives no first-audio latency. An L4 has about a third of a 4090's memory bandwidth, so at batch 1 expect an RTF well above 0.3. That could put the "10 active streams per L4" figure for STT plus TTS out of reach. The phase 1 benchmark must measure both TTS first-audio time and TTS streams per GPU.
3. **Chatterbox cannot share a Python environment with Ray Serve 2.59.** The `chatterbox-tts` 0.1.7 package pins `torch==2.6.0` and `gradio==6.8.0`, and gradio requires `starlette<1.0`, which conflicts with `ray[serve]==2.59.0`. Its pins are kept separate in `services/tts/requirements-gpu-chatterbox.txt`. Serving it needs its own image: no Ray Serve, an older Ray, or a loader written against the weights instead of the PyPI package. This is not a licence problem.
4. **Chatterbox watermarks every output.** All generated audio carries Resemble AI's imperceptible Perth watermark. That is probably acceptable, and arguably good for voice-cloning consent, but the product should disclose it.
5. **Newer open models exist that ARCHITECTURE.md does not mention.** Qwen3.5-4B (Apache-2.0, 4.66B, released 2026-02) succeeds Qwen3-4B. Gemma 4 E2B, E4B and 12B accept audio input and do speech-to-translated-text. Neither was substituted. Both are candidates for the phase 1 COMET and tok/s benchmark if the architect agrees.
6. **Urdu coverage is unchanged:** Whisper and MADLAD-400 cover Urdu. No permissively licensed TTS in this list does, as ARCHITECTURE.md already says.
